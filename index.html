      About jsbeeb
      By Matt Godbolt, based on Sarah Walker's b-em: huge thanks to her for open sourcing it, and to Richard Talbot-Watkins for his help and support. The Acorn Atom emulation is Andrew Hague's. The cycle-accurate emulation owes a great deal to the fantastic Visual 6502 project, who are well worth a donation. The disc loaded by default is the amazing Elite, made available by Ian Bell.
      Source on GitHub. For more on the BBC, and people to talk to about it, try the StarDot forums. Works best in Chrome or Firefox. Icons are from Bootstrap Icons (MIT).
      Privacy Policy & Terms of Service
      • jsbeeb uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies, to count how the site is used and to find what goes wrong. There are no server logs and nothing is kept on the jsbeeb servers; this is a static page served from an AWS bucket. What is sent:
        • the page's address, with anything of your own taken out: a disc from your Google Drive, this browser or your computer is named only by where it came from, and a shared session's room and names are removed;
        • each disc or tape loaded: where from, how it was picked, and whether it loaded or why not in general terms. Public discs are named; your own are not;
        • how the emulator started: the machine model, keyboard layout, whether it is embedded in another page, how it was asked to boot, and whether WebGL and sound are working;
        • which parts of jsbeeb are used, such as the media window, the debugger, rewind or Help;
        • errors in jsbeeb itself: the start of the message, with quoted text and addresses removed, and where in jsbeeb's code it happened;
        • shared sessions being hosted, joined, failing and ending, and roughly how many took part;
        • pressing Break, and a few of jsbeeb's shortcut keys.
        Not sent: what you type into the emulator, the contents of any disc or tape, or the names of your own files. Google Analytics also records what it records on any site, such as your browser, device type and rough location.
      • No personal information is kept. Logging in to Google Drive stores only what is needed to reach your own discs, and that is never shared with the jsbeeb developers.
      • Use of jsbeeb is at your own risk, for whatever lawful purpose you wish. Reasonable efforts are used to keep the site up to date and working.
      Would you rather run your own copy? The source is on GitHub.
